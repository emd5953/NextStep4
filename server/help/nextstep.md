# Using NextStep

NextStep helps people discover jobs, manage their profile, and keep a list of jobs they want to apply to.

## Accounts and profiles

Create an account using email and password, then confirm your email. Sign in to update your name, location, skills, photo, and resume. Profiles and resume files are private. Resume uploads accept PDF files up to 10 MB. Photos accept JPG, PNG, or WebP files up to 5 MB. Gemini can analyze a resume and suggest skills; review the suggestions before saving.

## Discovering jobs

Browse Jobs searches job listings. The home feed can recommend jobs based on your skills and location. If AI matching is unavailable, listings can still be shown without relevance scores. A new installation needs a job provider configured before listings appear.

## Saving and applying

Swiping right or choosing a job saves it to My Jobs. This does not submit an application, fill employer forms, or contact an employer. To apply, open the job's employer link from My Jobs and complete the application on that website. Automatic employer submissions are not implemented.

My Jobs keeps a snapshot of each saved listing, so its original details remain available. Add notes to track your own progress. Removing a saved job removes it from your active list; it does not withdraw an application sent to an employer. Skipping a job hides it from recommendations.

## Troubleshooting

If sign-in fails, confirm the email address and password. Request a reset link from the login page if needed. If resume analysis reaches its quota, save your profile and try the analysis later. If job listings are empty, the provider may be unconfigured or temporarily unavailable; this does not indicate a successful application.
